Menorca, Spain

Discover Menorca’s sandy beaches, turquoise coves and peaceful coastal scenery. Explore charming towns, enjoy seaside cafés and take time to relax. It is a destination for beach days, gentle walks and a slower pace of travel.

THINGS TO SEE AND DO

Explore Menorca at your own pace, combining time by the sea with visits to its towns and countryside. Spend a morning relaxing on a beach or discovering a sheltered cove, then wander through local streets and stop at a café. Enjoy coastal walks, browse small shops and try local food at a seaside restaurant. Leave time to watch the sunset and choose activities that suit the weather, your interests and the length of your stay.

Where to stay

Choose a guesthouse, budget hotel or self-catering apartment to suit your trip. Before booking, check the distance to beaches, shops and bus stops, especially if you are travelling without a car.

Entry requirements

Menorca is part of Spain. Check the official Spanish entry requirements for your nationality and travel documents before booking.

Getting around

Bus line 10 connects Menorca Airport with the bus station in Mahón, where you can connect to other island routes. Check current timetables before travelling, as services vary by season.
