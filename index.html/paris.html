THINGS TO SEE AND DO

Explore Paris at your own pace, combining famous landmarks with quieter neighbourhood discoveries. Enjoy views of the Eiffel Tower, walk beside the Seine and spend time exploring art at the Louvre or Musée d’Orsay. Wander through Montmartre, browse bookshops and stop at a café for a pastry. Leave time for a garden stroll or a relaxed meal, choosing the places and activities that suit your interests and the length of your visit.
