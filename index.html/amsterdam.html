THINGS TO SEE AND DO

Explore Amsterdam at your own pace, from its picturesque canals to the charming streets of the Jordaan. Spend time discovering art at the Rijksmuseum or Van Gogh Museum, then take a break in Vondelpark or a cosy café. Browse local markets, try Dutch pancakes and enjoy a canal cruise for a different view of the city. Whether you prefer museums, relaxed walks or discovering neighbourhood shops, you can choose the activities that suit your interests and the time you have.

Getting around

Take a train from Schiphol Airport to Amsterdam Centraal. The airport railway station is directly below the terminal. Check your route before travelling and explore the city on foot or by public transport.
