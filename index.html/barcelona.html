THINGS TO SEE AND DO

Explore Barcelona at your own pace, combining architecture, neighbourhood walks and time by the sea. Admire the Sagrada Família, discover the colourful spaces of Park Güell and wander through the narrow streets of the Gothic Quarter. Browse food markets, stop for tapas and enjoy a walk along the waterfront. Leave time to relax on the beach or explore local shops, choosing the activities that suit your interests and the length of your stay.
