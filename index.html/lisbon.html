Lisbon, Portugal

Discover Lisbon’s colourful streets, historic trams and beautiful river views. Enjoy local pastries, explore charming neighbourhoods and unwind in cosy cafés.

TRIP OVERVIEW

  • Estimated budget: £420 per person

  • Includes: Accommodation, food, local transport and activities
  • Excludes: Flights, visa fees and travel insurance

THINGS TO SEE AND DO

Discover Lisbon at your own pace, from the lively squares of Baixa to the narrow streets of Alfama. Enjoy river views from Praça do Comércio, stop for coffee and a pastel de nata, and explore the shops and cafés of Chiado. Head to Belém for historic landmarks and a waterfront walk, or visit São Jorge Castle for views across the city. If you have more time, consider a day trip to Sintra. Choose the places and activities that match your interests and the length of your stay.

Where to stay

Stay in Baixa or Chiado for a central base near cafés, shops and major sights. Choose a hostel for a lower-cost stay or a guesthouse or budget hotel for more privacy.

Entry requirements

Entry requirements depend on your nationality, travel documents and any valid visas or residence permits. Check official guidance before booking.

Getting around

Take the Metro’s Red Line from Lisbon Airport, changing lines for your destination. Explore by metro, bus, tram or on foot. Expect hills and cobbled streets.
