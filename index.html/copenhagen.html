THINGS TO SEE AND DO

Explore Copenhagen through its waterfront walks, neighbourhood streets and cosy cafés. Admire the colourful buildings around Nyhavn, browse design shops and spend time discovering museums or relaxing in a park. Try Danish pastries, enjoy a meal at a local food market and see the city from the water on a canal tour. Choose a few favourite activities and leave time to wander, whether you are visiting for a short break or a longer stay.

Getting around

Take the M2 metro from Copenhagen Airport’s Terminal 3 towards the city centre. The train is another option for Copenhagen Central Station. Check your route before travelling and explore the city on foot or by public transport.
